Submissions

I wanted to keep things as analog as possible. The intent was to capture the handheld, textural feel of objects/ephemera as a way to represent the intimacy and vulnerability of real connection.

I hoped to achieve this by limiting submissions to things that would be mailed in and then scanned and reproduced as authentically as possible within the zine.
